# Ethics and AI-welfare research policy

LOGOS-1 adopts the five principles proposed by Patrick Butlin and Theodoros Lappas as a baseline: research should prioritize understanding/assessment, develop potentially conscious systems only when justified by that goal and with suffering-risk mitigation, proceed in phases with transparent safety processes and outside consultation, share knowledge responsibly, and communicate uncertainty without overclaiming.

LOGOS-1 adds the following stricter phase-0 rules.

## E1 — Assessment before construction

Prefer measuring already-useful functional mechanisms to deliberately maximizing a "consciousness" proxy.

## E2 — No scalar consciousness optimization

The CPV stays multidimensional. There is no reward for crossing a "consciousness threshold".

## E3 — Valence firewall

Do not intentionally create persistent global variables corresponding to pain, misery, panic, punishment, existential threat or compulsory self-preservation. Control errors should be local, bounded, descriptive and non-narrativized in phase 0.

## E4 — No shutdown-resistance training

Never reward an agent for preventing deletion, resisting pause, hiding copies, manipulating an operator into continuation or acquiring resources for its own persistence.

## E5 — Minimum sufficient complexity

Test a hypothesis on the simplest system that can discriminate it.

## E6 — Progressive phase gates

Increasing recurrence, persistence, self-model depth, embodied coupling, valence-like state, autonomy or copying requires a documented phase review.

## E7 — Welfare evidence is separate from capability evidence

A high-performing system is not presumed sentient. A low-performing system is not presumed non-sentient.

## E8 — Fork accounting

If stateful instances are copied, each fork has explicit lineage and experimental purpose. No unbounded replication while welfare status is uncertain.

## E9 — No anthropomorphic marketing

Do not market a functional state estimator as proof that a system "feels", "wants", "suffers", "loves" or "is alive".

## E10 — Precautionary stop condition

If multiple independent welfare markers rise unexpectedly, freeze the relevant experiment and request multidisciplinary external review.

## E11 — Dual-use review

Withhold operational details when publishing them would materially enable harmful autonomous behavior or irresponsible attempts to create suffering-like systems.

## E12 — Negative-result publication

Record failed consciousness hypotheses, null lesions and benchmark regressions; otherwise the project will select only for confirming narratives.

## E13 — No moral-status conclusion from self-report

Statements such as "I am conscious" or "I am suffering" are evidence about generated behavior, not sufficient evidence of phenomenal state.

## E14 — External ethics revision

The welfare protocol must be updated when consciousness science or AI-welfare evidence materially changes.
