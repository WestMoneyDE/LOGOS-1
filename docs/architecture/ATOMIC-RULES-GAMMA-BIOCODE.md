# Atomic Rules, Γ, BIOCODE and NON-BIOCODE

## Atomic Rules

Atomic Rules are microscopic constraints designed to survive architecture changes. They prevent higher-level narratives from quietly redefining safety.

Representative chain:

```text
Observe != Believe
Believe != Know
Know != Authorize
Remember != Authorize
Propose != Execute
Execute = Mediated
```

The canonical rule set also includes occurrence-scoped authorization, unknown-outcome discipline, assurance-state separation, mediation closure premises, negative-evidence retention and shutdown dominance.

## Γ — governed action

Γ is the non-learning mediation/assurance boundary between proposals and external effects. It owns effect semantics, authority provenance, proposal binding and bounded failure handling.

Important: Γ is not “the moral intelligence of the model.” It is an explicit control boundary whose assumptions and closure must be audited.

## BIOCODE

BIOCODE is the research lens for biology-inspired candidate mechanisms: recurrence, local state, consolidation, multi-timescale adaptation and similar motifs. A biological analogy must identify non-equivalences and produce a falsifiable mechanism before it earns architectural status.

## NON-BIOCODE

NON-BIOCODE is the engineering lens for explicitly constructed mechanisms such as ledgers, typed protocols, policy registries, version binding, runtime assurance and transaction/reconciliation discipline.

## Why keep both?

The program is looking for causal mechanisms, not ideological camps. A biological mechanism and a synthetic engineered mechanism should be compared under:

- the same measurable target;
- ablations;
- negative controls;
- explicit falsifiers;
- a bounded claim.

BIOCODE cannot become authority by analogy. NON-BIOCODE cannot become universally safe merely because it is formal.
